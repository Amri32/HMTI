# HMTI UBSI Website

Product: website publik Himpunan Mahasiswa Teknologi Informasi Universitas Bina Sarana Informatika.

Audience: mahasiswa Teknologi Informasi, calon anggota, pihak kampus, masyarakat, dan calon mitra kegiatan.

Primary jobs: memahami tujuan organisasi, memeriksa visi dan misi, mengenali struktur, mengikuti program kerja dan berita, serta menemukan alamat dan kontak HMTI.

Public truth source: `app/site-content.ts`. It contains the verified organization name, university, establishment date and city, address, vision, missions, introductory copy, and public navigation.

Content boundary: program, news, leadership identities, photographs, and statistics are published only after verification. Temporary stock photographs must be visibly identified as illustrations and replaced with permitted HMTI documentation before final institutional publication.

Homepage action: help a first-time visitor understand HMTI and continue to program work, organization information, or contact details.
